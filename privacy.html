Privacy policy

Effective October 23, 2026. This policy describes the owner's private, personal-use Google integration and this public information website.

Google data accessed and why

The owner's authorized connection can read Gmail messages, selected Drive files, Google Docs, Google Sheets and calendar information to answer questions, summarize information and assist with personal work. Calendar permissions also support explicitly requested event changes. The personal Google connection does not request Gmail send permission.

Google account identity information identifies the authorized account. Access is limited to permissions the owner grants and the assistant features the owner requests.

Processing and sharing

Data is processed by the private assistant on owner-controlled computers. Requested content can enter conversation history and local assistant memory. If the owner deliberately selects an external model or external tool, content needed for that request may be sent to that selected service; this website is not involved in those requests.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to develop or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. Its use and transfer follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. No unrelated people are given account access through this website.

Storage, retention and deletion

Credentials are kept in protected local credential storage, never in this public website or its source repository. Retrieved information may persist in private chat history, local memory and private backups according to the owner's retention choices.

The owner can delete retained records and applicable backups through the private application and storage controls. Revoking Google authorization prevents future authorized requests, but does not itself erase previously retained local records.

Control and contact

The owner can review or revoke access through Google Account connections. For integration questions or deletion requests, use the developer support email displayed on the Google consent screen. The application is for personal use, not a public multi-user service.

This public website

This static website has no forms, scripts, analytics or application-set cookies, and does not collect or display Google user data. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary website request metadata under the GitHub privacy statement.

This policy will be updated if the integration's data practices change.
